On July 9, 2026, I remotely participated in the IEEE P1936.26 Kickoff Meeting and Outline Review, held in Hangzhou, China.

During the meeting, I took part in the review and discussion of the draft outline and contributed comments from the perspective of the standard’s technical scope and future development.

It was a valuable opportunity to participate in the early-stage discussion of the standard and exchange ideas with other experts involved in the working group.
