## IEEE P1936.26 Kickoff Meeting and Outline Review

On **July 9, 2026**, I remotely participated in the **IEEE P1936.26 Kickoff Meeting and Outline Review**, held in **Hangzhou, China**.

During the meeting, I participated in the review and discussion of the proposed outline and contributed comments to the development of the standard.

It was a valuable opportunity to take part in the early-stage technical discussion and exchange ideas with members of the working group.
