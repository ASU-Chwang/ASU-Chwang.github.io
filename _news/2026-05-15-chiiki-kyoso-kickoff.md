## Regional Co-Creation Kickoff Exchange Meeting

On **May 15, 2026**, I participated in the **地域共創キックオフ交流会 (Regional Co-Creation Kickoff Exchange Meeting)** hosted by **Aichi Sangyo University**.

The event brought together faculty members and participants from different academic backgrounds and provided an opportunity to learn about a wide range of research activities and topics.

## Learning about Diverse Research Topics

During the meeting, I attended presentations by faculty members and learned about their current research themes, ongoing projects, and approaches to connecting academic research with regional and social needs.

It was particularly valuable to hear research perspectives from fields different from my own. The discussions provided new insights into how researchers from different disciplines identify practical problems, develop research questions, and explore opportunities for collaboration.

## Academic Exchange and New Perspectives

For me, the event was also a valuable opportunity to broaden my academic perspective beyond my main research areas of medical imaging, artificial intelligence, and biomedical image analysis.

Participating in this exchange meeting allowed me to better understand the importance of **interdisciplinary communication and regional co-creation**, and provided inspiration for considering how advanced technologies and academic research can contribute to wider societal challenges.

I sincerely appreciate **Aichi Sangyo University** for organizing this valuable opportunity for research exchange and communication.
