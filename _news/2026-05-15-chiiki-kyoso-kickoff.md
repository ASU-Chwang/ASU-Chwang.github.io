## Regional Co-Creation Kickoff Exchange Meeting

On **May 15, 2026**, I participated in the **地域共創キックオフ交流会 (Regional Co-Creation Kickoff Exchange Meeting)** hosted by **Aichi Sangyo University**.

During the meeting, faculty members introduced their current research topics and activities. It was a valuable opportunity to learn about research areas outside my own field and to gain new perspectives through interdisciplinary academic exchange.

The event also provided an opportunity to consider how research and technology can contribute to regional and societal needs.
